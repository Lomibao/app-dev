## My favorite Movies
1. Fight club - It is about a man who struggles with insomnia and becomes friends with a mysterious man named Tyler Durden. Together, they create an underground fight club that eventually turns into something much bigger and more dangerous.
2. Cowboy bebop: The movie - It follows a group of bounty hunters aboard a spaceship who are chasing a dangerous terrorist threatening the planet Mars. Along the way, they uncover a deadly conspiracy involving a mysterious biological weapon.
3. 28 days later -  It is about a man who wakes up from a coma in an abandoned London and discovers that a deadly virus has turned most of the population into violent, infected creatures. He joins a group of survivors as they struggle to stay alive.
4. 28 weeks later - It takes place months after the original outbreak, when the military attempts to rebuild society in London after the virus appears to have been contained. However, a new outbreak begins, putting everyone at risk once again.
5. Drive - It is about a quiet and mysterious Hollywood stunt driver who works as a getaway driver for criminals at night. His life takes a dangerous turn when he gets involved with a woman and her troubled husband.
